Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Westman Printing.

How do I place an order?

Simply fill out our enquiry form with your requirements, or email us at quotes@westmanprinting.com.au. Our team will review your brief and get back to you within 24 hours with a personalised quote and timeline.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept PDF, AI (Adobe Illustrator), PSD, EPS, and TIFF formats. For best results, please supply print-ready PDFs with 3mm bleed, crop marks, and fonts embedded or outlined. Our team is happy to guide you through file preparation.

What are your minimum order quantities?

Minimum quantities vary by product: Digital printing from 50 units, offset printing from 500 units, packaging from 500 units, and promotional items from 100 units. Contact us for specific requirements on specialty products.

Do you offer design services?

Yes, we offer a full design and pre-press service. Our in-house designers can create artwork from scratch or adapt your existing files for print. Design fees are quoted separately based on project scope.

What is your turnaround time?

Standard turnaround is 5–10 business days from artwork approval. Rush options are available for urgent jobs. Delivery timelines depend on quantity and product type — we'll confirm the exact schedule in your quote.

Are you ISO certified?

Yes. Westman Printing holds ISO 9001 and HACCP certifications, ensuring every product meets the highest quality and safety standards. Our quality management system covers every stage from pre-press to dispatch.
